The fourth mod in the All-Bamba series adds turrets for every electrical tech level: a machine gun, an EMP gun, a rocket launcher and an artillery piece at each of the Industrial, Spacer and Ultra tiers, plus a charge anti-air turret with Combat Extended.

Every turret has its own research project in the **All-Bamba Tech** research tab. Use the switch above the tables to see the stats with or without Combat Extended.

## Compatibility

Works on RimWorld 1.6 and 1.7 and needs no other mod. With these installed it changes as follows:

- **Combat Extended**: every turret uses CE ammo, magazines and recoil. The Ultra tier fires its own 8x40mm Ultra Charged rounds, plasma cells and plasma shells. The charge AA turret is CE only.
- **Muzzle Flash**: the turrets get muzzle flashes.
- **Turret Pipeline** (Reel): turrets reload from the pipeline's turret ammo instead of steel or plasteel.
- **Guns For All Post-Medieval Tech Levels**: with Combat Extended, the Ultra charge rounds use its charge damage, which wears armor down twice as fast.
- **Beyond Our Reach**: the turrets slot into BOR's tier ladder, Industrial < Spacer < Solar < Ultra < Quasar < Tenebral. Ultra turret research comes after the Solar defender and costs 120,000; Ultra turrets cost 780 plasteel and 35 advanced components and have 1,250 hit points (artillery 2,000).

The tables above show the mod on its own, without these changes.
